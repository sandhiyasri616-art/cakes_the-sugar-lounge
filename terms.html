Terms & Conditions

1. Orders and Payments

All custom cake orders require a 50% non-refundable deposit to secure your booking date. The remaining balance is due on or before the date of delivery or pickup.

2. Cancellations

Cancellations made more than 7 days prior to the event will forfeit the deposit but will not incur further charges. Cancellations within 7 days of the event require full payment.

3. Liability

Once a cake or baked good has been picked up or delivered, The Sugar Lounge is no longer responsible for any damage that may occur. Please drive carefully and store cakes in a cool, air-conditioned environment.

4. Allergies

While we take extreme care to prevent cross-contamination, our kitchen handles nuts, dairy, gluten, and eggs. We cannot guarantee that our products are 100% free of these allergens.
