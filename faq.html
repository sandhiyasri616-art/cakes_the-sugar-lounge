Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place a custom cake order?

You can place a custom cake order by navigating to our 'Custom Cakes' page and filling out the enquiry form. We recommend placing custom orders at least 1-2 weeks in advance.

Do you offer vegan or gluten-free options?

Yes! We offer a select range of vegan and gluten-free bakes. Please specify your dietary requirements when placing an enquiry so we can accommodate your needs.

How do the baking classes work?

We offer both Beginner and Advanced hands-on baking classes at our studio. All ingredients and equipment are provided. You can book your slot directly through our 'Baking Classes' page.

Do you deliver?

We offer delivery within city limits for a small additional fee based on the distance. Alternatively, you can pick up your order directly from The Sugar Lounge.
